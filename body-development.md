# Preliminary physical body development

## Concept target
A modular spherical research platform inspired by the OMNIKOSMOS concept image. Initial planning target: approximately 250 mm outer diameter. This is a provisional target, not a finalized engineering dimension.

## Proposed modules
1. **Outer shell:** two or more removable sections for access and maintenance.
2. **Central electronics bay:** mounting plate for a controller and low-voltage electronics.
3. **Sensor window:** unobstructed location for camera or optical sensor, if later selected.
4. **Status ring:** decorative/indicator lighting only in the first build; it is not an electromagnetic propulsion system.
5. **Power bay:** physically separated, secured low-voltage power source with fuse and accessible disconnect.
6. **Thermal and cable paths:** ventilation and strain relief based on actual component heat and cable needs.
7. **Mounting interface:** a stable stand for the stationary first prototype.

## Suggested development sequence
- Make a cardboard/foam mock-up to verify size and access.
- Choose real components and obtain their dimensions and power requirements.
- Build a CAD assembly with clearances and fastener locations.
- Print or fabricate an unpowered shell and check fit.
- Install low-voltage electronics only after checking insulation, heat, stability, and cable strain relief.
- Test sensors and software while the device remains stationary.

## Important design constraints
- Do not seal heat-producing electronics without thermal analysis.
- Do not use exposed high-current coils or high-voltage components in the first prototype.
- Keep batteries protected, mechanically secured, and disconnectable.
- Any underwater version requires a separately engineered pressure/waterproof enclosure; an ordinary 3D-printed shell is not automatically watertight or pressure-safe.
- A ring around the sphere does not itself create propulsion or dimensional transport.

## CAD status
No validated CAD file or manufacturing drawing is included yet. This document is an initial design brief; dimensions, tolerances, materials, fasteners, and parts must be selected and verified before fabrication.
